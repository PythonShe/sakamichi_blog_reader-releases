## About

**Sakamichi Blog** is a free, fan-made app that lets you read official blog posts from three Sakamichi Series idol groups:

- **Nogizaka46** (乃木坂46)
- **Sakurazaka46** (櫻坂46)
- **Hinatazaka46** (日向坂46)

Browse the latest posts, discover your favorite members, and save the posts you love — all in one place.

## Features

- **Blog Feed** — Browse the latest blog posts in a beautiful card layout. Pull to refresh and scroll to load more.
- **Member Profiles** — View member details including birthday, generation, birthplace, blood type, zodiac sign, and social media links.
- **Oshi (Favorite Members)** — Long-press any member to mark them as your "oshi". Your favorites appear in a dedicated section for quick access.
- **Bookmarks** — Save blog posts you want to read again. Bookmarked posts are collected in one page.
- **Search** — Quickly find members by name with real-time search.
- **Group Switching** — Seamlessly switch between Nogizaka46, Sakurazaka46, and Hinatazaka46.
- **Download & Export** — Save blog content to your device. Export cached posts as HTML files with images.
- **Dark Mode** — Full support for light, dark, and system-default themes.
- **Multi-Language** — Available in English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

## Privacy

Sakamichi Blog is **local-first**. All your data — browsing history, bookmarks, settings — stays on your device. The app does not collect, store, or send any personal data to external servers. Network requests go directly to the official group websites to fetch blog content, just like a web browser would.
