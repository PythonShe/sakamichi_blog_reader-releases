## 3. Data Collection and Accounts
The App does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to developer-operated servers. The developer does not operate a backend service, account system, login, cloud sync, or analytics tracking for the App.

## 4. Local Storage
Your reading history, favorites, app settings, and cached content are stored only on your device. This information is used only to provide the App's local features.

## 5. Deleting Local Data
You can remove app-local data by uninstalling the App. The developer cannot access or delete data stored only on your device. Images or files that you explicitly save to the Photos app or export to another folder are managed outside the App and are not deleted when you uninstall the App.

## 6. Network Requests and Third Parties
To load content and parsing rules, the App makes direct network requests from your device to official group websites and GitHub/CDN endpoints. Those services may receive your IP address, user agent, and other standard request metadata under their own policies. The developer does not proxy, intercept, or log these requests.

## 7. Photos Permission on iOS
If you choose to save an image on iOS, the App requests Photo Library Add permission only to save that image to your Photos library. The App does not read, import, or scan your existing photo library contents.
